Methods & references

Pre-processing

Spike removal uses a locally adaptive MAD (Median Absolute Deviation) test to flag points that deviate from their neighbourhood by more than a configurable multiple of the local MAD (Hampel 1974). Detected spikes are replaced by linear interpolation from the nearest clean neighbours. Savitzky-Golay smoothing fits a local polynomial (order 2 or 3) in a sliding window by least-squares (Savitzky & Golay, 1964; Rinnan et al. 2009), preserving peak positions and shapes while reducing random noise — the standard filter for reflectance spectroscopy.

Vegetation indices

29 indices across six functional groups: greenness/biomass (NDVI, EVI, SAVI, MSAVI2, GNDVI), chlorophyll & N-status (CIre, CIg, MTCI, NDRE, TCARI, MCARI), red-edge dynamics (REP, RVSI, mND705), stress & carotenoids (PRI, SIPI, PSRI, ARI1, ARI2), canopy water content (WI, NDWI, MSI, NDII), and nitrogen/dry matter (CCCI, DD, RDVI — VIS-NIR only, 400–900 nm; NDNI, NDLI, CAI — require SWIR). Indices report N/A if required wavelengths fall outside the loaded spectral range, so multispectral sensors limited to 400–900 nm still get usable N and biomass proxies via CCCI, DD and RDVI.

PROSAIL forward model

PROSPECT-5 (Jacquemoud & Baret 1990; Féret et al. 2008) computes leaf optical properties from Cab, Car, Cbrown, Cw, Cm and leaf layer count N. 4SAIL (Verhoef 1984; Verhoef et al. 2007) folds these into a canopy bidirectional reflectance factor given LAI, leaf angle distribution, hotspot and acquisition geometry. Both are implemented in JavaScript, validated against the reference prosail Python package to <10−8 reflectance units.

LUT inversion

Monte-Carlo sampling generates a large random parameter ensemble; each entry is simulated with PROSAIL. The observed spectrum is matched against this LUT by RMSE over available wavelengths. All entries within 1.2× the minimum RMSE are averaged to give the retrieved parameter and its spread. A k-NN fallback (25 nearest) is used when the threshold yields too few matches. Default parameter ranges (editable under Advanced) come from literature surveys of crop canopies (Wang et al. 2022; Zhou et al. 2017; Duan et al. 2014).

Solar position calculator

Solar zenith and azimuth are computed with the NOAA solar position algorithm (Meeus-based), accurate to within a few hundredths of a degree. City search resolves coordinates via OpenStreetMap Nominatim; the UTC offset is then estimated from longitude (15° ≈ 1 hour) as a starting point — always verify it against the actual time zone and daylight saving for your flight date.

Caveats

  • LUT inversion suffers equifinality: Cab, Car and N are correlated. LAI, Cbrown and soil terms are generally more reliably retrieved.
  • Narrow parameter ranges using prior knowledge (crop, phenological stage) dramatically improve accuracy.
  • Acquisition geometry must match your measurement; canopy BRF is angle-dependent.
  • This is a research/teaching tool — validate against ground measurements before operational use.
